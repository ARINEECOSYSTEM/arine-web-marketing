# Unpublished announcement concept

Proposed headline: ARINE in the Spotlight: Advancing the Future of D2C Growth Technology.

Status: draft only; not loaded by the website.

Do not state that ARINE appeared in a top SaaS business magazine without the
actual publication name, original article URL, publication date and verified copy.
Do not add media logos, awards or third-party endorsements without evidence.

The reusable news-card template accepts `headline`, `date` (ISO date), `summary`,
`category` and `href`. Add only verified updates to `content/news.json`.
