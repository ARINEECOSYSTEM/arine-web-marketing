What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites and web apps you visit. They are widely used to make software work efficiently, remember user preferences, maintain active login sessions, and protect against automated security exploits.

Under the EU ePrivacy Directive, GDPR, and CCPA, we provide clear notice and controls regarding all first-party and third-party cookies utilized across our platform.

How to Control & Opt-Out of Cookies

You can control and block cookies through your internet browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies.
  • Analytics Opt-Out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
Note on Essential & Payment Cookies: Blocking essential or payment security cookies (Stripe/Razorpay/Dodo) may cause subscription billing failures or prevent logging into your SaaS workflow portal.
