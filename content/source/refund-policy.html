01How to Cancel Your Subscription

You may cancel your Arine SaaS subscription at any time without penalty. Self-service cancellation is available directly in your dashboard:

  1. Log into your Arine merchant dashboard at https://development.arineecosystem.com.
  2. Navigate to Settings & Billing > Subscription Management.
  3. Click Cancel Subscription and confirm.
Cancellation Timing: Cancelling your plan turns off automatic billing renewals. Your automated workflows and store API integrations will remain operational through the end of your current paid billing period.

03Payment Gateway Refund Timelines & Methods

Approved refunds are credited strictly to the original payment method used during checkout. Arine does not issue cash or check refunds.

International Cards

Refunds reflect on card statements within 5 to 10 business days depending on your issuing bank.

UPI & Domestic Cards

UPI refunds credit back to VPA within 24 to 48 hours; Netbanking/Cards take 5 to 7 business days.

Cross-Border Transactions

International cross-border transactions process within 5 to 7 business days with full localized tax refunding.
