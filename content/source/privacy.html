Privacy Policy

How Arine handles account, Meta advertising, store, creative, and billing data.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Operator: Arine Ecosystem Pvt Ltd

Contact: support@arineecosystem.com

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Arine Ecosystem Pvt Ltd ("Arine", "we", "us") provides a platform that helps businesses research, plan, create, and manage advertising campaigns, including Meta campaigns, and connect their e-commerce stores. This policy applies to our public website, dashboard, integrations, and related services.

For account, billing, and website data, Arine generally acts as the data controller. For store, campaign, and customer data that a business connects to Arine, we generally act as a processor or service provider on that business's instructions.

3. Facebook and Meta integration

Arine connects to Meta through Meta Login and the Meta Marketing APIs. After you authorize the connection, Arine may receive the Meta ad account IDs available to you, campaign and performance data, Facebook Page and Instagram account names and IDs, access tokens, and the permissions you grant.

We use this Meta data only to display your eligible assets, prepare advertising plans, create or update campaigns on your instructions, and report campaign performance. Campaigns and other advertising objects created by Arine are created paused. You decide whether to activate them and control the budget and spend in Meta.

If the app requests pages_read_engagement, it uses that permission only to retrieve Page identity and engagement information needed to select the correct Page, prepare relevant campaign context, and report on connected Page performance. We do not publish Page content using that permission.

4. Service providers and connected platforms

The following providers may process data for us and only to provide their contracted service or act on your instructions:

  • Our application hosting and database providers — host the Arine website, application, database, and operational logs.
  • Cloudflare — stores and delivers uploaded or generated images and protects network traffic.
  • Apify — performs competitor and public-web research requested through the service.
  • Pollinations and OpenRouter — process prompts, relevant brand context, and images needed to generate campaign text or creative output.
  • PayU — processes payments and payment-related fraud checks.
  • Google — provides Google sign-in and returns your authorized name, email, and account identifier.
  • Meta — provides Facebook, Instagram, and advertising APIs when you connect your account.
  • Shopify and WooCommerce — provide store data when you connect a store.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we use contractual and other lawful safeguards for international transfers.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and brand data: while your account is active and for up to 30 days after a verified deletion request.
  • Meta and store data: while the integration is connected or the related project remains in your account. Disconnecting Meta immediately revokes and deletes the stored Meta access token.
  • Uploaded images, generated images, and chats: until you delete them, close the account, or request deletion; deletion is completed within 30 days, including from active storage.
  • Security and application logs: normally up to 90 days, unless longer retention is required to investigate abuse or comply with law.
  • Invoices, transaction records, and tax records: up to 8 years, or the longer period required by applicable Indian tax, accounting, or legal rules.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted, access-restricted backups for up to 90 days before being overwritten.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete your personal information. You may also withdraw consent and disconnect Meta or a store at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights and may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Email support@arineecosystem.com from your registered email address with the subject “Privacy Request.” We may verify your identity before acting and normally respond within 30 days. See our dedicated Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Legal bases, security, and children

We process data as needed to perform our contract with you, with your consent when you connect an account, to comply with law, and for legitimate interests such as security, support, and service improvement where those interests do not override your rights.

We use HTTPS in transit, encrypt access tokens at rest, and limit access to personnel and service providers who need it for their work. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

The service is intended for businesses and people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service or law changes. We will post the revised policy here and change the “Last updated” date; material changes may also be communicated in the app or by email.

Questions or rights requests: Arine Ecosystem Pvt Ltd, support@arineecosystem.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.
